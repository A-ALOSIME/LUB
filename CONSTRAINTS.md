- Arabic RTL, keyboard operation, visible focus, and no horizontal overflow at 320/768/1024/1440 pixels. Check in a real browser.
- Missing service credentials must never enable a fake session or persist fake student records.
- External service integration is unverified until tested against that service. Report that boundary explicitly.
